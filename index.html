Certificate of Analysis (COA) Disclosure

Certificates of Analysis (COAs) may be available for certain products upon request. Unless specifically stated otherwise, these COAs are provided to us by our suppliers and represent testing associated with products or batches supplied by those vendors.

We do not independently perform, commission, or pay for the testing represented by supplier-provided COAs. We also do not independently test every product, vial, or batch received.

We work with vendors that provide products represented as meeting their stated specifications and may make the corresponding supplier documentation available to our customers for research reference and transparency.

By relying on supplier testing rather than commissioning independent testing for every batch, we are able to reduce testing expenses and offer products at more accessible prices for research and experimental applications.

Supplier-provided COAs should not be interpreted as independent verification performed by us, nor do we represent that a COA independently verifies every individual unit sold.
